Textile & Garments

From greige to finished fabric.

Bangladesh’s textile and garment sector depends on consistent wet-processing chemistry. Alongside caustic soda and hydrogen peroxide, our own SAMSOF, SAMWET, SAMPER, SAMSEQ, SAMCRE and SAMNEU auxiliaries cover every stage of processing.

  • Scouring
  • Bleaching
  • Mercerizing
  • Dyeing
  • Neutralizing
  • Softening & finishing

Brightness, strength and efficiency.

Water Treatment

Safe water for communities and industry.

Municipal and industrial water operators rely on effective disinfection and pH control. Our chlorine, hypochlorite, hydrogen peroxide, acids and alkalis support drinking-water, process-water and effluent systems.

  • Disinfection
  • pH control
  • Resin regeneration
  • Effluent treatment
