About Samuda

A Bangladeshi manufacturer with a long-term view.

Samuda Chemical Complex Limited (SCCL) is an industrial chemical manufacturer and a member of T.K. Group. We focus on delivering dependable products, responsive service and technical know-how to customers in Bangladesh and international markets.

Our people are protected by a documented framework of 54 policies — from labour rights and equal pay to health, safety and anti-corruption.
