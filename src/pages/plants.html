01

Chlor-Alkali Plant

Salt, water and electricity — turned into essential chemistry.

Industrial raw salt is dissolved in water and purified. The concentrated brine is then electrolyzed to produce caustic soda, chlorine gas and hydrogen.

The plant uses ion-exchange membrane technology, delivering superior product quality with low energy consumption and no pollution.

02

Hydrogen Peroxide Plant

Clean oxidizing power, up to 250 MT a day.

Samuda technical-grade hydrogen peroxide is the most economical grade for industrial applications in which inorganic stabilizers are accepted.

It bleaches chemical and mechanical pulp, recycled paper, and natural and synthetic fibers; acts as a desizing agent; safely detoxifies industrial wastes such as phenolics and reduced sulfur compounds; and is used in metal extraction and finishing. It decomposes to water and oxygen with no toxic residues.

04

Samuda Power Limited

Our own power. Uninterrupted production.

Samuda Power Limited (SPL), a sister concern of T.K. Group of Industries, generates and distributes power to Samuda Chemical Complex — one of the largest caustic soda and hydrogen peroxide plants in Bangladesh.

The natural-gas-fired plant at Gazaria, Munshiganj, near the river Meghna, runs three Wärtsilä (Finland) reciprocating engines and was founded in 2009.
