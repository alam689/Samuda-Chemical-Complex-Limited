Quality & Sustainability

Quality you can measure. Responsibility you can trust.

Consistent products, safe operations and care for the environment are central to how we manufacture.

Quality assurance

A disciplined approach to quality.

Quality is built into each stage of production — not inspected in at the end.

Incoming inspection

Raw materials are sampled and verified against specification before use.

In-process control

Key parameters are monitored throughout production.

Finished-goods testing

Each batch is analysed in our laboratory before release.

Traceability

Batch documentation and retained samples for every delivery.

Product safety

Clear information for safe use.

We provide product-specific Safety Data Sheets and handling guidance so customers can store, use and transport our products safely.

  • Safety Data SheetsAvailable on request for each product and grade.
  • Labelling & packagingContainers labelled with product and hazard information.
  • Customer guidanceAdvice on storage, handling and protective equipment.

Certificates & compliance

Details of our current certifications and compliance documents, including scope and validity, are available to customers on request. Please contact our team and we will share the relevant documents.

Sustainability

Responsible chemistry for a greener tomorrow.

We are committed to reducing the environmental footprint of our operations and supporting the communities around us.

Water stewardship

Responsible water use and treatment of process effluent at our facility.

Cleaner products

Ion-exchange membrane electrolysis with low energy use and no pollution, and products such as hydrogen peroxide that break down to water and oxygen.

People & community

Safe workplaces governed by 54 documented policies, and positive relationships with our neighbours in Gazaria.
