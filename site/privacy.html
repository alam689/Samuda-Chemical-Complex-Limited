Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

Who we are

This website is operated by Samuda Chemical Complex Limited (“Samuda”, “we”, “us”), T.K. Bhaban, 13 Karwan Bazar, Dhaka-1215, Bangladesh.

Information we collect

  • Inquiries and quotation requests: your name, company, email, phone number, country, delivery location and details of your requirement.
  • Job applications: your contact details, CV and any information you choose to include.
  • Inquiry list: products you add to your inquiry list are stored only in your own browser until you submit a request.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry, prepare quotations, process orders, assess job applications and keep appropriate business records. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Your information is accessible only to authorised Samuda staff who need it, and to service providers who help us operate this website, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. Job applications are retained for a limited period and then deleted.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting info@scclbd.com.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
