About Samuda

Chemistry for a better tomorrow.

Samuda Chemical Complex Limited is an industrial chemical manufacturer based in Bangladesh, supplying essential chemicals and textile auxiliaries to the industries that drive the national economy.

Our story

Rooted in Bangladesh. Built for industry.

Established in 2006, Samuda Chemical Complex Limited (SCCL) is a concern of T.K. Group, one of Bangladesh’s established industrial groups.

From our manufacturing facility at Sikirgaon, Gazaria in Munshiganj, we produce basic industrial chemicals, textile auxiliaries, specialty chemicals and water-treatment products. Our commercial and corporate teams operate from T.K. Bhaban in Karwan Bazar, Dhaka.

Today Samuda is one of the largest caustic soda and hydrogen peroxide producers in Bangladesh, with chlor-alkali, hydrogen peroxide and calcium carbonate plants supported by our own 28.19 MW power station.

Our approach is simple: make dependable products, test them carefully, deliver them on time, and stand behind our customers with practical technical support.
