Overview

About SAMWET BLN

SAMWET BLN® is a biodegradable, low-foaming wetting and scouring agent. Its emulsifying and detergency properties make it versatile for scouring and wetting, efficiently removing waxes, oils, grease, dirt and most stains from cotton and blends and making textiles hydrophilic.

Special features

  • Environment friendly
  • Very low foaming — suitable for high-turbulence machines
  • Outstanding hydrophilicity on treated fabrics
  • Increases absorbency and whiteness in peroxide bleaching and pretreatment baths

Handling & safety

Use face shields, protective clothing, gloves and boots when handling. Avoid contact with skin and eyes. If splashed in the eyes, rinse immediately with plenty of water for 10–15 minutes and seek medical advice. On skin contact, wash immediately with soap and water and remove soiled clothing.

Always refer to the product Safety Data Sheet (SDS), available on request.
